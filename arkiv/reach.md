# Reach

Garuda serves owners and prospective buyers of WiseOne Signature NFT stakes. The implemented Arkiv feature is the marketplace's default discovery source, so users who browse listings or purchase bids use it directly. It is not a separate sponsor-only demo.

At verification on 6 November 2026 (India time), the Garuda namespace contained two available listings (Signature Stakes #20 and #40) and one bid (Signature Stake #20), from two distinct Ethereum maker addresses and one dedicated Arkiv publisher. These are integration observations, not a count of daily users or paying customers. [Reproduce the entity query](schema.md#query).

No independently verified active-user, retention or volume metrics have been supplied for this submission. We do not infer reach from wallet balances, testnet entities or the wider WISE community. The team should supply measured product analytics if available.

The feature is implemented and locally verified. Public deployment and a post-Mumbai release date have not been established here. The intended audience after the event is the same stake owners and buyers, with Arkiv discovery included in their existing listing and bidding flow. Advanced trading and lending are not claimed as launched features.
