Garuda is being built around transferable NFT stakes. The aim is to connect owners with potential buyers and lenders, so they can explore new ways to use an existing position.

The application we are building

Three connected parts, introduced in stages.

01 / MARKET

Discover a stake

Browse positions, compare asking prices, and review a stake before buying or placing a bid.

02 / MY STAKES

Manage your position

See your NFT stakes, follow their maturity, and prepare a listing when you want to find a buyer.

03 / LENDING

Explore borrowing

Request a loan against a stake, or offer funding for a specific position with clear loan terms.
